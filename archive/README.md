# Original research files

`original/Initial_guess.m` and `original/Nonlinear_iteration.m` preserve the supplied MATLAB files byte for byte. They are research provenance, not the recommended entry point for running this repository.

Use `src/solve_nonlinear_pde.m` and `examples/demo_internal_layer.m` for the revised implementation. The boundary corrections, changed convergence checks, and execution limitations are documented in [the validation note](../docs/validation.md).

The research poster is in [docs/research-poster.pdf](../docs/research-poster.pdf). Full team credits appear in the main README. No individual role or new license is inferred from these materials.
